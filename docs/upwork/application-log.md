# Upwork Application Log

Use this after every submitted proposal. Keep the proposal version and proof asset stable long enough to learn from the result; change one variable at a time after each five-application review cycle.

| Date | Job | Score | Connects | Proof Used | Proposal Version | Viewed | Interview | Offer | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Review Cycle

After five applications, calculate:

- proposal-to-view rate
- view-to-interview rate
- interview-to-offer rate
- Connects spent per interview
- which proof asset was used most often
- which job lane produced the strongest response

Change only one variable for the next cycle:

1. opening sentence
2. proof asset
3. job lane
4. bid structure
5. scope question

## Rate Review

Keep the profile rate at `$33.33/hour` during the launch cycle. Reassess after several completed contracts, strong reviews, or an interview rate that supports moving upward.
